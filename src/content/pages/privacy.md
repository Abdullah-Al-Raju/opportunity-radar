**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

Welcome to **Opportunity Radar** (accessible at `https://opportunity-radar-c60.pages.dev`). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Opportunity Radar and how we use it.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

Opportunity Radar is designed as a privacy-friendly, static publishing platform. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profiles to browse our scholarship guides and developer tool resources.

However, certain standard technical data may be automatically logged when you access our site:
- **Log Files**: Following standard hosting procedures on Cloudflare Pages, our servers automatically log visitor requests. This includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These logs do not contain personally identifiable information and are strictly used to analyze trends, administer the site, and maintain uptime.
- **Cookies & Web Beacons**: Opportunity Radar and our third-party advertising/analytics partners may use cookies to store information about visitor preferences and past interactions to optimize page loading speeds and personalize content delivery.

---

### 2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) to serve ads based on users' visits to Opportunity Radar and other sites on the internet.
- Users may opt out of the use of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- We may partner with authorized third-party ad networks and ad servers that use cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Opportunity Radar. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and personalize ad content.

---

### 3. Affiliate Links & Partner Tracking

Opportunity Radar participates in educational and developer affiliate programs. When you click an affiliate link on our site to explore an external partner service (such as international student banking, scholarship preparation materials, or developer tools), the partner website may place a temporary referral tracking cookie on your device. This allows the partner to credit Opportunity Radar with a referral commission at zero additional cost to you. We do not collect or store any financial or purchasing data from our partners.

---

### 4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance Rights

We respect the privacy rights of visitors worldwide, including under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- **Right to Access & Deletion**: You have the right to request information regarding any personal data we hold or request its deletion.
- **Right to Opt-Out**: You have the right to opt-out of cookie-based tracking and targeted advertising through your browser settings or ad network consent banners.

---

### 5. External Links Disclaimer

Our articles contain links to official university portals, government scholarship agencies, grant foundations, and developer tools. Once you navigate away from Opportunity Radar to an external third-party domain, our Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.
