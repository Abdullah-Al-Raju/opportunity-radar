**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

Welcome to **Tech & AI Radar** (accessible at `https://opportunity-radar-c60.pages.dev`). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Tech & AI Radar and how we use it.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

Tech & AI Radar is designed as a privacy-first, static developer intelligence platform. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal profiles to browse our AI benchmarks, architecture breakdowns, and developer tool guides.

However, certain standard technical data may be automatically logged when you access our site:
- **Edge Log Files**: Following standard hosting procedures on Cloudflare Pages, edge servers automatically log visitor requests. This includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and edge cache status. These logs do not contain personally identifiable information and are strictly used to maintain uptime and mitigate DDoS attacks.
- **Cookies & Web Storage**: Tech & AI Radar and our third-party advertising/analytics partners may use cookies to store information about visitor preferences and theme settings (such as dark/light mode) to optimize page loading speeds.

---

### 2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on users' visits to Tech & AI Radar and other sites on the internet.
- Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- We may partner with authorized third-party ad networks that use cookies or web beacons in their respective advertisements. These technologies measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and personalize ad content.

---

### 3. Affiliate Links & Partner Tracking

Tech & AI Radar participates in developer and cloud affiliate programs. When you click an affiliate link on our site to explore an external partner service (such as cloud credits, GPU instances, or AI IDEs), the partner website may place a temporary referral cookie on your device. This allows the partner to credit Tech & AI Radar with a referral commission at zero additional cost to you.

---

### 4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance Rights

We respect the privacy rights of visitors worldwide, including under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- **Right to Access & Deletion**: Visitors can request details about any personal data held or request its deletion.
- **Do Not Track**: We respect browser-level Do Not Track signals and Global Privacy Control (GPC) headers.
