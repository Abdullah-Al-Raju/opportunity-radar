### Editorial Response Time

Our editorial team reviews messages daily. We aim to respond to inquiries within **24 to 48 business hours**.

If you are an educational institution, scholarship foundation, or developer tool creator looking to have your program featured or verified on Opportunity Radar, please include:
1. Official program or tool name.
2. Official portal URL (.edu, .gov, or verified organization domain).
3. Eligibility criteria, application deadlines, and funding amount.
4. Official contact information for verification.
