### Editorial Response Time

Our engineering and editorial staff reviews messages daily. We aim to respond to technical inquiries and developer partnership requests within **24 to 48 business hours**.

If you are an AI lab, developer tooling startup, or open-source maintainer looking to feature your project or benchmark on Tech & AI Radar, please include:
1. Official project or tool name and GitHub repository.
2. Documentation URL and API / SDK reference.
3. Architecture details, license (MIT, Apache 2.0, open-weights), and benchmark results.
4. Contact details for technical verification.
